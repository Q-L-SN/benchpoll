Contribution & Community Rules

Compare models using traceable evidence and relevant benchmarks. Debate the measurement, not the person.

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026

These rules cover benchmark and model contributions, scores, weighting and discussions on BenchPoll. They supplement the Terms of Use; information handling is described in the Privacy Policy and Cookie & Local Storage Policy. They do not expand any user's access to private data.

  • Use the same task context when deciding which benchmarks and weights are appropriate.
  • Support scores with a checkable source and the relevant model, benchmark version and run configuration.
  • Reasoned criticism of a benchmark, vendor, methodology or ranking is welcome. Fabrication, manipulation, harassment and privacy violations are not.

1. Choose for the same context

BenchPoll compares models using selected benchmarks, versions, task/context options, weights and fallback rules. First choose the relevant task and options, then select benchmarks that measure that task under compatible conditions. Explain why a benchmark belongs there. Do not mix incompatible contexts merely to produce a preferred winner or describe a context-specific result as universal capability.

Personal Weights express your priorities, not a new benchmark measurement. Public Weights aggregate users' saved personal pies in public categories for the relevant context; they are not staff certification, a popularity endorsement or objective ground truth. Private categories do not contribute to Public Weights, and their names and settings are not public discussion material or used for research. Authorized operators can still access them to operate, support and secure the service. See the Privacy Policy.

Do not create deceptive accounts, coordinate spam votes, buy or sell weighting influence, use automation to evade limits, or otherwise game Public Weights. A legitimate disagreement about benchmark relevance is not abuse. See How it works before interpreting the ranking or the scores used.

2. Make scores and claims verifiable

  • Identify the measurement: select the correct model and benchmark version. Preserve material model identity, reasoning effort, harness and harness version, tool access, token budget and other run conditions where relevant and supported by the source. Do not guess missing settings or invent values to fill a field.
  • Provide evidence: use the existing source and notes fields to link the original leaderboard, paper, repository, dataset or reproducible evaluation. Include the relevant result, scale/unit, date and methodology context when available. Clearly distinguish a vendor-reported result, an independent test and your own run; disclose a material affiliation or incentive in the existing notes or discussion.
  • Keep the reported value honest: do not fabricate, tamper with, selectively relabel or misattribute scores; alter a scale without explanation; hide relevant failures; or pass off estimates, ranking positions or screenshots without provenance as verified measurements. Link the underlying evidence whenever available.
  • Check for duplicates: search existing models, benchmarks, versions and results before submitting. Correct or request a merge rather than resubmitting the same result under a new spelling. A new vendor name, source snapshot or fetch date alone does not establish a different evaluation.

You may submit original evaluation results if you have the right to share them and describe their limitations. Approval is not an independent reproduction or a guarantee of accuracy. Criticism of a claimed result is welcome when it identifies the evidence and the specific concern.

3. Benchmark versions and dynamically synced scores

A benchmark version represents a meaningful evaluation difference, such as task data, protocol or scoring method. Explain how a proposed version differs and why its results are or are not comparable. Do not present a dated source snapshot or a routine leaderboard refresh as a new benchmark version, or merge genuinely different versions just to pool their scores.

For a dynamically synced benchmark version, manual new-score submissions are disabled. Use the existing score-details correction/report action where available, or feedback to report an incorrect result, source mapping, stale import or sync problem. Do not work around the restriction by creating a duplicate benchmark, fake version or copied manual score. A correction to a synced score must not silently move it to a different version.

For a broader source or batch, use Contribute and its Request bulk import entry, or the plain-text feedback form. Link official sources, identify the benchmarks, models and versions to include, and explain any access or reuse restrictions. This is a request for review, not permission to scrape or a promise to import every row.

4. Submission rights and public/private boundaries

Account-linked contributions, saved weights and discussion posts require an eligible authenticated account and applicable current Terms acceptance. You must be at least 16 and meet applicable legal eligibility requirements; GitHub sign-in requires a verified email. Verification is not real-name verification, proof that a score is correct, or permission to expose someone's email.

Only submit material you are entitled to share and authorize BenchPoll to use as described in the Terms. A public URL does not override a third-party license, copyright, database right, confidentiality duty or access restriction. Attribute the source and respect its license. Do not paste protected benchmark questions, licensed datasets or source text when a lawful citation and factual summary suffice. Respect trademarks and do not imply that a model vendor, benchmark owner or logo endorses BenchPoll.

Ordinary discussions are public with the displayed account identity. Approved benchmark/model information, score evidence and object notes can become public. Review-only comments, feedback and private reports are not automatically public posts; publication of a corrected benchmark fact does not make an entire private report public. Pending items are subject to the existing permissions, not a promise of confidentiality or publication.

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, confidential evaluation data or unnecessary personal information. Do not disclose private-category contents or another person's identity, email, location or private correspondence without a lawful basis. Authorized operator access needed to run and secure private features is distinct from making them public.

5. Keep disagreement useful and safe

Benchmark validity, contamination, sampling, statistical uncertainty, comparison fairness, model marketing and BenchPoll's own methods are all legitimate topics. Link evidence, distinguish facts from opinion, acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes. A low score, strong criticism or an unpopular weighting choice is not by itself grounds for removal.

Do not post threats, targeted harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, impersonation, doxxing, unlawful material, repetitive promotion or spam. Do not fabricate evidence or use misleading vendor claims, coordinated accounts or deceptive endorsements to distort discussion. Do not solicit credentials, exploit vulnerabilities, bypass access controls or intentionally disrupt the service.

Use a discussion's report action where available, the private report form for a discussion report, or feedback for other concerns. Provide the relevant post/object ID or link, a concise reason and supporting evidence. Do not republish harmful private information to prove that it was disclosed; describe the location and use the private route.

6. Review, moderation and outcomes

The BenchPoll operator and authorized reviewers may check sources, request clarification, correct or consolidate records, reject or remove content, and restrict accounts for data quality, safety, privacy or legal reasons. Some permissions differ by role. An item appearing in a queue or receiving an acknowledgement does not guarantee approval, immediate review or adoption of a suggestion.

Moderation should address the specific content or conduct, not suppress evidence-backed criticism merely because it is inconvenient to a vendor or the operator. Relevant review decisions and actions can be recorded for integrity and accountability under the Privacy Policy. Removal of a public post need not erase every audit record or reply placeholder.

Where available, review status appears in the contribution or account interface. A service email notification may be available, but receipt or delivery is not guaranteed and no marketing consent is inferred. Where applicable law requires reasons, notice, a response deadline or a particular review process, those requirements take precedence.

7. Corrections, challenges and rights concerns

Factual correction, duplicate or merge
Use the existing edit/correction entry for the affected object, or benchmark correction. For an unavailable action, multiple objects or a merge, use feedback. Identify the current record, proposed correction, evidence and information that should be preserved.
Challenge a review or restriction
Use feedback with the request, object or post ID, the decision you question and any missing evidence. There is no guaranteed dedicated appeal button or outcome. If sign-in is unavailable, use the private request form to reach the operator and explain the access problem without trying to bypass the restriction.
Copyright, trademark, confidentiality or privacy
Send a private concern through the request form, available without sign-in. Identify the affected URL/content, your relationship to the rights involved, the issue and requested action, with only necessary evidence. Do not submit passwords or unnecessary identity documents. The operator may seek proportionate clarification; filing a request is not automatic removal or a legal determination of ownership. No designated statutory copyright agent or special notice procedure is promised here.

These routes do not require a public accusation, change form visibility or waive any statutory complaint, appeal or court remedy. Sensitive material should stay out of public discussions. The request form records a case for human handling; it does not itself send an email, guarantee a reply's delivery or automatically execute deletion.

8. Changes and operator contact

We will date updates and give appropriate notice of material rule changes where required. These rules do not retroactively authorize disclosure of private information, replace third-party licenses or remove mandatory legal rights.

For private contact with the BenchPoll operator, use the request form. Read the Terms, Privacy Policy and cookie policy for the related service and information-handling rules.
