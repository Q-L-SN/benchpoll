Topic proposals

When 30 other people support a proposal, administrators review it for publication.

  • Up to 3 active supports and 1 collecting proposal per account.
  • Collection lasts 14 days. Review and closed proposals do not use these limits. Private Topics are unaffected.
  • While collecting, you can cancel support or edit or withdraw your own proposal. Updating a proposal resets its support count and keeps the original deadline.
  • Submit a saved private Topic with its scenes and weights. Everyone can preview and discuss the proposal; the proposer controls its weights while collecting support.
