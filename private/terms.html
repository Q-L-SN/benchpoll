2. How rankings work

BenchPoll combines published or contributed scores using the evaluation items, weights, fallback rules and comparison settings selected for a topic or scene. Personal pies define your evaluation choices and weights. Public Weights represent community priorities applied to benchmark measurements.

Rankings depend on the available evidence and the selected methodology. Missing scores represent gaps in the evidence. Estimated score includes calculated estimates and applies a measured-coverage adjustment; Score range fills missing results with 0 at the lower end and 100 at the upper end, keeping measurements fixed. Fallback selects the first available result in the applicable order. Different versions and model settings are distinct evaluation conditions. See the source evidence and calculation explanation for details.

Topic references are dynamic: a public index uses the target's Public Weights; a personal index uses your saved target weights where available and otherwise the target's Public Weights. References to topics with scenes identify a specific scene. Updating a referenced configuration can change dependent indices without modifying their saved reference or allocation.

Community AGI thresholds reflect the selected category and weights. Use the original sources and your own evaluation needs to assess a model's suitability.
