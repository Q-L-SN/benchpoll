How BenchPoll works

Define Super Intelligence, democratically.

Compare AI models by topic or scene. Benchmark results supply the evidence; community or personal weights define what matters.

Browse Topics

Start with a topic

Open a topic for its public index, a model for its results and sources. Personal weights let you build your own index.

Filters apply to every card; Estimated score is the default. Browse Popular, Recent, or Mine.

A topic may have one level of scenes with separate weights and discussions. The default scene comes first.

Expand topics to browse scenes; scene search matches appear immediately. Scenes are evaluation goals. The default does not average the others.

Make the ranking yours

Add benchmarks or topic references from Evaluation items to Personal Weights. Index composition totals 100% when nonempty. With two or more items, select a block and adjust its weight; other shares rebalance.

Try it without signing in. Sign in to save your weights. Unsaved changes are marked on the page.

Community priorities

Public Weights combine saved personal weight configurations in this topic, shaped by recent activity. Editing yours updates your contribution, not the number of votes.

Older configurations remain eligible to fill sparse samples up to 10 total.

Each topic or scene has its own Public Weights. Another topic contributes only through an explicit reference. Private topics and their weights never contribute to Public Weights.

Build an index from other topics

You can add another topic as a weight item, choosing a specific scene if it has scenes. References follow live weights, not copies. Changing a default scene does not retarget existing references.

Public indices always follow public target weights. Personal indices prefer your saved target weights and otherwise follow public weights. Private topics may reference your other private topics; public-topic configurations cannot reference private topics.

Weights multiply along reference paths and combine for each benchmark version. Different fallback choices retain their shares. Scores and coverage correction are calculated after expansion; child scores are never averaged.

References combine capabilities; they do not intersect requirements. Combining real-time image generation and vector generation does not by itself measure real-time vector generation.

Circular references are rejected. Empty or unavailable targets retain their missing weight and show a notice, rather than increasing other weights.

Scores and coverage

Both modes use the same qualifying measurements, converted using each test's score range and direction, then combined using your weights. Fallback is resolved first.

  • Estimated score: combines measured results with shared-test estimates. Isolated extreme results have less influence on estimates; incomplete measured coverage reduces the total, not just its estimated part.
  • Score range: missing scores count as 0 at the lower end and 100 at the upper end; measured scores stay fixed. Ranks by the lower end. Not a confidence interval.

Coverage is the share of weight with measured results, including successful fallbacks. Estimates do not increase coverage. Unresolved gaps are left out of Estimated score by default. Without a real result on a weighted test, a model cannot rank.

Coverage factor: f(c)=5c/(1+4c), with measured coverage c. Nonnegative totals use f(c), negatives 2-f(c); full coverage is unchanged.

Test algorithm applies only to this tab. Missing-only methods keep measured contributions fixed, bound predictions to 0–100, and start unresolved gaps at 50. This midpoint is a trial assumption, not an empirical estimate.

Unadjusted keeps that missing contribution; Rational multiplies it by f(c); Linear by c; Square root by √c. Midpoint blends it toward 50 using c and 1−c, so scores can rise or fall. All five stay within Score range; full coverage is unchanged.

Turn on effort or harness to rank settings separately; otherwise use each model's best qualifying score per test. Missing settings stay in an unknown group.

Fallback: backup results for missing scores

Added benchmarks follow the community. Open Fallback to choose a saved order or edit your own. No fallback turns backups off; deselect your configuration to follow the community again.

Only explicit public choices updated within 14 days shape the mix, weighted by benchmark allocation. Following adds no vote. If nobody has chosen, the index works normally without backups.

Try the chosen version first, then backups in order. The first available score counts, not the highest, including zero and negative scores. No fallback disables backups, not estimation.

Backup versions are selected explicitly. New versions never join an existing order automatically.

Your account

Propose a configured private topic through the hall. Its scenes and saved weights become a frozen snapshot. Anyone can preview, try weights, or save a private copy. Replacing the snapshot resets support.

Support up to 3 collecting proposals and author 1. Collection lasts 14 days; 30 other supporters trigger administrator review. Private topics and scenes are immediate, editable, and visible only to you.
