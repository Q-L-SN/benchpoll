Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Storage keeps you signed in, remembers your workspace and helps you return to a draft.

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Wording updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains storage used by the BenchPoll operator on benchpoll.com. Read it with the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Contribution & Community Rules. Cookie rules can also apply to local storage and similar technologies.

  • BenchPoll's application sets a first-party sid cookie for sessions and security, not a separate analytics or advertising cookie.
  • Browser storage supports requested features. Some records last for a tab session; persistent preferences have no application-set expiry unless stated below.
  • Non-essential tracking is not enabled by default. There is no "accept all cookies" banner for the current session and requested-feature storage; this notice is not consent to future tracking.

1. Session and login security

sid is a signed session identifier sent to BenchPoll, with the session contents held on the server. It is HTTP-only, has SameSite=Lax, uses the site-wide path and is marked Secure for HTTPS connections. Page scripts cannot read this HTTP-only cookie. An untouched new session is not saved: reading this policy or the public policy-information endpoint does not itself create a session cookie.

Sign-in, login preparation and privacy-form security can create or update a session, including when you open privacy controls without signing in. The CSRF token used by those forms and GitHub OAuth state are held in the server-side session, not separate CSRF or OAuth cookies and not local-storage credentials. OAuth state is accepted for at most ten minutes and is removed when the callback is processed; an abandoned state may remain in the server session until that session is replaced or expires.

Duration: the cookie and server-session lifetime use the operator's configured SESSION_MAX_AGE_DAYS value, converted to days in milliseconds. This persistent cookie can keep you signed in after you close the browser. Your browser's cookie details show the expiry actually issued; session updates can renew it. Logout, session revocation or account controls can end access earlier. Expired server sessions are cleaned up on the configured periodic schedule; backup retention follows the Privacy Policy.

Use account and privacy controls for available session actions. Deleting the cookie prevents that browser from presenting its identifier; it does not itself revoke every other device or erase the account.

2. Workspace state and draft recovery

sessionStorage is scoped to the site and browser tab. It survives ordinary reloads and same-tab navigation. It normally ends when that tab or window session ends, but browser restore and duplicate-tab behavior can preserve or copy it.

Guest mixes and account-owned recovery drafts
Representative prefixes are benchpoll:guest-pie:v3: and benchpoll:owned-pie:v1:. They can hold benchmark selections, weights, fallback rules, category/context identifiers and a pending save needed to recover your work. Recovery records are scoped to their guest or account context, not made public by being stored. Relevant records are removed when a matching draft is discarded or its save is acknowledged, where those actions apply; failed or unconfirmed saves can leave recovery records for the tab session. They have no timed automatic expiry.
Workspace and navigation choices
benchpoll-workspace-preferences-v1 remembers Public/Personal mode, score mode and fallback expansion; benchpoll-sidebar-preferences-v1 remembers sidebar view, scroll and expansion. These session records are updated as you use the relevant controls and have no additional application-set time limit.
Unconfirmed write protection
benchpoll:unconfirmed-write: stores a retry identifier under a digest of the request, so retrying a save need not duplicate it. The matching entry is removed after a validated successful response or certain identity errors; uncertain failures may retain it for the tab session. A recovery draft can separately contain the pending request's contents.

Local drafts stay in your browser. Submitting or saving a mix sends the relevant data to the server. Saved personal pies in public categories contribute to aggregated Public Weights; private categories do not. Private categories are not used for research, even if you previously opted in. Authorized operators can still access them to operate, support and secure the service; see the Privacy Policy.

3. Persistent preferences and comparison profiles

localStorage is shared by same-origin tabs in the same browser profile and can remain after the browser closes. The records in this group have no application-set automatic expiry. They remain until overwritten, deleted by an applicable feature, removed by you or evicted by the browser.

Requested comparison settings and profiles
benchpoll-comparison-settings-v1: and benchpoll-comparison-profiles-v2: remember comparison options and the last configuration for the guest or account identifier. Guest named configurations are stored locally; signed-in named profiles are stored with the account and the browser retains a local last-configuration cache. Deleting a named configuration removes that named entry, not every other preference or server record.
Display and dismissed welcome
benchpoll-workspace-display-v1 remembers the selected workspace display. benchpoll-welcome-seen-v1 is written when you dismiss the welcome, so it need not reappear on each visit. Clearing it can show the welcome again. These are feature preferences, not permission for analytics.
Sign-in change signal
user-profile-update records a timestamp after the login callback to tell other tabs to refresh account state. The timestamp can remain until overwritten or site storage is cleared.
Recent topics
benchpoll-sidebar-recent-v1: remembers up to 80 successfully visited topic/context entries and visit times for the Recent list. Guest and account histories use separate keys. It does not store names, avatars or page contents, and the history is not sent to the server or counted as topic heat. Account-scoped entries can remain after sign-out on the same device until removed or site storage is cleared.

People with access to your device or browser profile may inspect local records, including account and category identifiers. On a shared device, sign out and remove the site's storage when finished.

4. Contribution form handoffs

benchpoll:contribution-entry: records carry the selected object, source page and any supplied prefill details to a contribution form, including a form opened in another tab. Rendering a contribution link only prepares data in memory: it does not persist the handoff. The persistent record is prepared when you activate the link (including a middle-click or opening its context menu), or use an explicit control that opens the form.

The local-storage handoff has a 30-minute validity period. Expired or malformed local handoffs are removed on later handoff activation, not by a continuously running deletion timer. An expired entry can therefore remain physically stored until later cleanup or you clear it. Reading a handoff copies the details into sessionStorage so reload/back navigation can recover the form; it does not immediately delete the local copy. That tab copy has no separate 30-minute timer and can remain for the tab session.

A legacy contribution URL can also create a tab-local handoff. Review the prefilled form and submit it when ready. The server checks permissions and validates your submission. Keep secrets, credentials and unnecessary personal information out of contribution and feedback details.

5. Providers, security logs and tracking

BenchPoll does not run advertising, behavioral-advertising cookies or a default independent analytics tracker. Fonts and application scripts are served locally. The current Content Security Policy blocks the observed external Cloudflare Web Analytics script and external analytics connections; same-origin scripts and requests remain permitted, and HTTPS images, including remote avatars, are allowed.

Cloudflare also provides proxying and network protection. It can process IP addresses, request metadata and security logs even if an analytics script is blocked. Provider security challenges may use their own storage depending on the deployed configuration. See Cloudflare's privacy notice and the provider disclosures for details.

GitHub sign-in happens on GitHub, which may use its own cookies under its own policy. Following a source or social link also takes you to that third party's policies. BenchPoll site-data controls do not clear cookies on those other sites.

Resend is used for service/review notifications, not to enroll you in marketing. BenchPoll notification templates do not add an open-tracking pixel. Resend documents that open and click tracking are disabled by default and configured at domain level; the operator must keep them off for these notifications. Provider delivery, failure and bounce records can still exist without open/click tracking. Receiving a notification, accepting the Terms or using this website is not marketing consent.

6. Clearing, blocking and separating your choices

  • Sign out or revoke sessions: use the available account controls to end server sessions. Logout clears sid for that browser but does not run a blanket local-storage or session-storage wipe. Closing or deactivating an account is not automatic complete erasure.
  • Remove browser site data: use your browser's settings for benchpoll.com to remove both cookies and stored site data. Cookie-only deletion need not remove local storage; deleting history or cache alone need not remove either. Browser interfaces differ, so check the selected data types.
  • Remove tab-local records: use developer tools to clear this site's session storage in each relevant tab, or close the tabs and avoid restoring that session. Also clear persistent local storage if you want to remove handoff copies and remembered preferences. Open or restored pages can hold in-memory state or write new feature records when used.
  • Block storage: browser settings can restrict cookies and site storage. Reading public pages remains possible, but sign-in, privacy-form CSRF protection, saved preferences, prefill and draft recovery may fail. Disabling JavaScript or using private browsing is not a request to erase server records.
  • Server-held information: clearing the browser does not delete saved weights, private categories, approved contributions, public discussions, moderation records or provider logs. For access, correction, erasure or an objection, use the private request form, available without sign-in. A human handles requests; submission is not automatic erasure.

Necessary session/security storage and storage for a feature you request are not repurposed as consent to advertising or optional research. Any future non-exempt tracking must remain off until the required prior choice is obtained, with a way to withdraw it. An update to this policy alone cannot provide that consent.

7. Changes and contact

We will update the date and explain material changes to storage purposes or providers before they start where required. Legal exemptions depend on purpose, necessity and applicable law. Your statutory rights remain in effect.

For questions about storage or a rights request, contact the BenchPoll operator through the private request form. Do not post session identifiers, private-category names or personal request details in public discussions.
