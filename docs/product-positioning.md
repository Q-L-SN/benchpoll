# BenchPoll Product Positioning

Revised 2026-10-09. Internal strategy, not a feature list to paste into marketing.

## English

### Strategic Choice

**Question leaderboard relevance to earn attention. Deliver immediate value through personal rankings. Build public priorities through real participation.**

A useful personal tool and a community-shaped public ranking are not competing products. A first visitor must get value before a large community exists. Recorded results serve both; choosing personal utility as the initial activation path does not discard the data advantage or the public mission.

The shared idea is that rankings depend on which benchmarks count and how much. BenchPoll lets people choose benchmarks for a task/context, set weights, and inspect the resulting ranking and its evidence. Eligible saved choices also contribute to Public Weights under the published rules.

### Message Hierarchy

1. Acquisition hook: **Better at the benchmark. Better for you?**
2. Immediate promise: choose benchmarks for your task, set their weights, and see your own AI ranking.
3. Community extension: eligible saved choices help shape Public Weights; discussion examines which benchmarks deserve to count.
4. Trust: show sources, versions, run configurations, coverage and calculation boundaries.

An alternate hook is **The best AI. According to whom?** Compare these openings using the same task and product flow, not two unrelated landing experiences. Small non-randomized social experiments are directional evidence, not statistically clean A/B tests. The existing Public headline, **Which AI benchmarks should count?**, still fits the product. This revision does not change homepage copy.

Benchmaxxing and selective disclosure are concerns to investigate, not unsupported accusations against named labs. Even accurate scores may not match a user's task priorities. Reweighting cannot repair contaminated data or replace independent testing.

### Cold Start And Operations

- Choose one initial task based on verified coverage and understandable versions, not catalogue size.
- An empty Pie and unfamiliar benchmarks still impose friction. Offer an explicitly editorial example in the demo; do not claim a one-click starter exists without checking.
- Help real model-selection users first. Separately invite benchmark readers and maintainers to critique evidence.
- Start on the maker's existing X account. Show a task, benchmark choices, weights, a useful personal result and its limitations; then offer optional saving/public participation.
- A demo can end at personal value. Do not force every visit through an empty Public view or treat personal choices as merely unpaid voting.
- Use Reddit only after checking local rules. Prepare Product Hunt once first-use works. HN requires human-written participation; do not provide generated posts/comments.
- Measure completed first-value experiences and later return usage first. Separately track eligible saved contributors, substantive discussions and verified corrections.
- Increase public-led content when actual recurring contributions support meaningful summaries, not at an arbitrary total-user milestone. Do not fabricate participants or seed consensus with undisclosed accounts.

### Boundaries

Weights express task priorities, not scientifically validated benchmark quality. Discussion likes do not directly become weights or measured scores. Guest previews are not public contributions; eligible saved mixes participate under current rules, while private-category mixes must not be advertised as automatically public.

Report the scope, dates and contributors behind public summaries. Distinguish weight changes from source updates, coverage and rolling participation when explaining rank changes. Never present inferred results as measured scores, imply source endorsement, or promise unverified share/import/analytics features.

## 中文

### 核心决定

**用榜单适用性的争议吸引注意，用个人排行榜立即兑现价值，再通过真实参与积累公众选择。**

个人工具和公众榜不是必须二选一的产品。即使还没有大社区，第一个人也应该得到价值。已有成绩同时服务两者，以个人使用作为启动路径，并不损失数据优势，也不放弃公众使命。

统一的观念是：排名取决于哪些基准应该纳入、各占多少权重。BenchPoll 让用户按具体任务和上下文选基准、调权重，查看自己的排名和依据。符合规则的已保存选择也会参与公众权重。

### 传播层次

1. 获客切入：**更会考高分，就更适合你？**
2. 即时承诺：为当前任务选基准、调权重，查看自己的 AI 排行榜。
3. 社区延伸：符合规则的保存选择参与公众榜；讨论哪些基准值得计入。
4. 信任依据：展示来源、版本、运行配置、覆盖和计算边界。

备选切入是：**最强 AI，谁说了算？** 两个开头应使用相同任务和体验流程比较，而不是变成两种互不相干的落地体验。小样本、非随机的社交传播只能判断方向，不包装成严格 A/B 实验。现有 Public 标题“Which AI benchmarks should count?”仍适合产品内。本次不改首页文案。

benchmaxxing 和选择性披露可以作为调查的问题，不能没有具体证据就指控某家厂商。即使分数真实，也不一定符合用户的任务偏好。重新加权不能修复被污染的数据，也不是独立实测。

### 冷启动与运营

- 按覆盖质量和版本可解释性选一个首发任务，不用数据库总条数代替可用性。
- 空 Pie 和陌生基准仍有门槛。演示提供明确标注为编辑示例的起点；未验证前不承诺一键套用。
- 首先帮助真正需要选模型的人，另外邀请评测读者、维护者审视证据，两者邀请不同。
- 从已有 X 账号开始，展示任务、基准选择、权重、个人结果和局限，再可选引导保存与公众参与。
- 演示可以停在个人价值，不必强迫每次经过空公众榜，也不把个人选择当作投票前的义务劳动。
- Reddit 先查版规；首次体验稳定后再集中做 Product Hunt。HN 要求真人自行撰写，不提供生成的帖子或评论。
- 优先统计真正获得首次价值的人和后续回访，单独统计有效保存贡献者、实质讨论和核实的更正。
- 当真实持续贡献足以支撑有内容的总结，再增加公众导向内容，不设任意的总用户数门槛。不虚构参与者，不用未披露账号制造共识。

### 边界

权重表达任务重要性，不是经过科学验证的基准质量。讨论点赞不直接换算成权重或模型实测分数。游客预览不算公众贡献；符合当前规则的保存配置才参与，不能把私人分类配置说成自动公开参与。

公众总结注明范围、日期和参与人数。解释名次变化时区分权重变化、来源更新、覆盖和滚动窗口。不把推断当实测，不暗示来源背书，不承诺未核实的分享、导入或统计能力。
