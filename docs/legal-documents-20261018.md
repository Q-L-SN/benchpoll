## What Changed

- Terms now explain express account agreement, 16+ eligibility and any required
  guardian authorization; free service status; context-dependent ranking and
  missing-score limits; personal/public aggregation and manipulation rules.
- Contributions keep their owners' rights. The non-exclusive operating permission
  is limited to necessary service use and intended visibility, respects third-party
  benchmark rights, and does not authorize publishing private data or unrelated use.
- Moderation, reasons, human review, corrections, rights complaints, service changes,
  suspension, closure, settings preservation and mandatory remedies are addressed.
  There is no mandatory arbitration, class-action waiver, paid obligation, unlimited
  indemnity, invented court or claim of a designated copyright agent.
- Privacy separates data sources, requested-feature uses, security/integrity, legal
  duties and consent-based research. It explains verified GitHub email, public fields,
  small-aggregate risks, browser storage, infrastructure, Resend and manual rights work.
- Both documents remove the personal GitHub identifier/profile. The neutral label
  is explicitly not a verified legal identity. The private unsigned form remains
  the published contact route; no untested privacy email address is advertised.
- Research remains default-off, optional and withdrawable without losing private
  categories. Its scope is unchanged: unmet evaluation needs and public taxonomy
  ideas from private-category names/configurations, not public weighting, unrelated
  sensitive inference, identifying disclosure or a general AI-training license.
- Category moves, merges or removal must preserve personal weights/fallbacks
  privately, without overwriting another private mix or creating a new public vote.

## Open Operator Responsibilities

### Identity and Contact: Not Resolved

The operator has not approved a public legal name or contact address and does not
want a personal GitHub name or profile published. Do not infer or disclose one,
restore the old profile, or ask again as part of this work. A neutral description
and functioning form do **not** resolve an applicable identity-disclosure duty.

PIPL Art. 17's identifiable name and contact particulars are distinct from an ID
number or household address. For GDPR, first decide Art. 3 applicability before
applying Art. 13. Any address requirement must be assessed under the specific
operator/business rules that actually apply, not declared globally mandatory.
The internal gap remains open pending an operator-approved lawful arrangement and
qualified assessment; a policy sentence cannot waive it.

The unsigned form is real intake, not an independently verified response channel.
Assign a senior handler to review the queue daily and arrange a proven private
reply method. An inbox/domain/DNS entry or successful outbound Resend test does not
prove that a privacy/support forward receives mail. Publish an email address only
after separately confirming receipt, response, failure handling and monitoring.
If required notice, contact or another lawful prerequisite cannot be met, assess
restricting or pausing the affected processing instead of claiming completion.

### Providers, Bases and International Flows: Not Verified

Record the actual contracting entities, roles, data, locations, subprocessors,
retention and agreements for AWS Lightsail, GitHub, Cloudflare and Resend. Do not
assume all four have the same processor/controller role. Include mainland-China
administration, local database copies/backups, cloud storage and onward recipients;
Ohio hosting does not locate every operation in Ohio.

Complete any applicable processor/DPA terms, transfer impact or PIPL impact
assessment, required transfer mechanism or documented exception, recipient notice
and separate consent. Nothing here verifies an adequacy entitlement, standard
contract, certification, representative or DPO appointment. Also document the
necessity of each account field and any legitimate-interest balancing; PIPL has no
general legitimate-interest basis. Public personal content may need a separate
permission. Do not infer that clicking Post or accepting Terms settles every case.

Assess EU/UK and other regional laws on actual scope and thresholds. Do not promise
an EU/UK representative, a California opt-out interface or any other unimplemented
regional mechanism merely because the service is accessible worldwide.

Resend has been tested for outbound review notifications. The live worker was not
enabled at handoff. Parent controls activation and historical-queue handling;
this task does not authorize delivery or imply that every queued notice will be
sent. Public policies deliberately omit internal outbox IDs and delivery quotas.
Notification templates are service-related, not marketing, and add no tracking
pixels. Provider metadata processing and an inbound privacy channel remain distinct.

Cloudflare DNS/proxy/security and network logs remain disclosed. The enforced CSP
excludes the reviewed external analytics-script origin, but does not guarantee that
all same-origin analytics or image-based tracking is blocked. Production network
verification remains the parent's responsibility; no real-edge probe ran here.

### Retention and Human Requests: Not Fully Implemented

The policy gives actual criteria and does not invent a universal deletion timer.
Approve a shortest-necessary schedule and accountable handler for account records,
weights/private categories, profiles, discussions/reports, audit payloads, legal and
choice evidence, request verification, notification contents/errors, logs and backups.
Account inactivity, research withdrawal and a closed case are separate events.

For each request, retain the received time/reference privately, determine the
applicable deadline and verify ownership proportionately. The internal 28-day marker
is neither a universal legal deadline nor a purge timer. A supplied email/reference
does not prove ownership. Do not demand passwords or identity documents by default,
disclose another user's data, or force GitHub sign-in/reactivation for a request.

An authorized human must investigate, perform and verify the approved remedy,
explain specific lawful exceptions, arrange recipient follow-up where required and
reply through a verified private route. Recording a status/resolution does not send
mail or erase records. Missing automation does not excuse an unmet rights duty.

Deactivation ends sessions and removes saved comparison profiles; it is not full
erasure. Re-login reactivates. Research withdrawal stops new optional analysis but
does not automatically unwind completed work. Review identifiable retained inputs
and results for deletion. Address audit/export/backup copies, document restricted
retention and disposal, and test that restoration does not reintroduce erased data.

Copyright and inaccessible-account appeals use the private form's Other option as
a fallback. The human handler must triage them; the form is not a designated DMCA
agent or automatic notice/counter-notice system. This rewrite does not establish a
statutory safe harbor or implement a new appeal dashboard.
